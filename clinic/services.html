Our services

Comprehensive care, close to home.

Two clinics working as one — general medicine, surgery, maternity, and specialist care for every stage of life, backed by a 24×7 emergency unit and pharmacy.

Women's Health & Maternity

Pregnancy care, normal & cesarean delivery — with normal delivery always our priority — infertility treatment and full gynecological care.

General Medicine

Fever, high BP, diabetes, thyroid disorders, chest & gastric infections, malaria, typhoid, dengue and seasonal illness.

Liver, Kidney & Urinary Care

Fatty liver, hepatitis, jaundice, cirrhosis, pancreatitis, gastritis, kidney stones, burning urination and prostate enlargement (BPH).

Neurology & Pain Management

Migraine, dizziness, epilepsy, paralysis, stroke, back pain, sciatica, arthritis, joint pain and other nerve disorders.

Pharmacy
